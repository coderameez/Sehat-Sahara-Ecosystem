## 🇵🇰 Built for the people behind every healthcare journey

A son trying to find the right doctor for his mother.

A family forwarding a blood request from one WhatsApp group to another.

A young doctor searching for an opportunity to put years of education into practice.

A clinic managing appointments, queues and patient information through scattered tools.

Different people. Different struggles. One healthcare system.

**Sehat Sahara starts with a simple belief: getting help should feel less difficult, and nobody should have to navigate healthcare alone.**

We are building a shared platform where finding care, providing care, managing facilities and helping the community can become parts of the same journey.

---

## 💚 What does “Sehat Sahara” mean?

**Sehat** means health.  
**Sahara** means support.

Together, they represent the kind of experience we want to build: practical support when someone needs care, clarity or a helping hand.

In Pakistan, looking after someone’s health is often a family effort. Children arrange appointments for parents. Friends search for blood donors. Relatives help find a trusted doctor.

That care already exists.

**Our mission is to help it reach people more easily.**

---

## 🧩 One ecosystem, four dedicated experiences

Healthcare involves more than the person booking an appointment. It also involves the doctor providing care, the facility coordinating it and the people responsible for trust and accountability.

Sehat Sahara brings these perspectives into one product vision.

| Experience | Who it serves | What it brings together |
|---|---|---|
| 📱 **Patient** | Individuals, families and caregivers | Finding care, appointments, records, reminders and community support |
| 🩺 **Doctor** | Doctors and healthcare providers | Availability, appointments, consultation workflows and professional opportunities |
| 🏥 **Hospital / Clinic** | Facility owners and operational teams | Doctors, patients, bookings, queues, services and schedules |
| 🛡️ **Master Admin** | Platform administrators | Provider review, facility accounts, user management and platform oversight |

The Patient and Doctor experiences live within the main user app. Hospitals/clinics and administrators have dedicated desktop dashboards.

## 🏥 Hospital & clinic dashboard
### More time for care. Less confusion around it.

The facility frontend demonstrates a shared workspace for daily operations:

- Dashboard overview
- Doctor and patient management
- Appointments and booking actions
- Queue and check-in interfaces
- Departments and services
- Schedules
- Billing-related screens
- Clinic profile and settings

### How facility onboarding is intended to work

1. A hospital or clinic submits an onboarding request through our website.
2. The Sehat Sahara team contacts the facility to understand its needs.
3. The team configures the facility workspace.
4. Authorized staff receive account access.

This is a team-assisted onboarding model. The current website demonstrates the request interface; automated provisioning and production account delivery belong to the next stage.

A focused space for blood requests and donor participation.

A space for sharing or borrowing useful healthcare items, such as mobility aids.

## 🤖 AI with a clear purpose

Health information can be hard to understand, especially when someone is anxious or unfamiliar with medical terms.

Our planned AI layer will help users:

- Describe their concerns in familiar language
- Understand general health information
- Organize information before seeking care
- Navigate relevant services within the platform
- Recognize situations that need professional or urgent attention

### Language should make care easier

Our language vision includes:

**English · اردو · Roman Urdu**

Language coverage is still evolving and needs testing with real users.

### Current AI status

The present build contains AI-related interface demonstrations. **Live AI is not integrated yet.**

Before real-world use, health-guidance features will need clinical review, evaluation, privacy controls and clear escalation rules. AI will support care navigation and understanding; it will not replace qualified medical judgment.

## 🔗 Why bring everything together?

A booking becomes more useful when the doctor and facility can act on it.

A record becomes more useful when the right professional can access it with permission.

An opportunity becomes more useful when a suitable provider and facility can find each other.

A community request becomes more useful when it reaches someone who can respond.

**Our product hypothesis is that shared workflows can create more value than isolated features.**

The next stage is to test that hypothesis through working integrations and real pilots.

## 🔬 Research that leads to better decisions

Sehat Sahara’s direction is informed by Pakistan’s healthcare context and questions around:

- Access to relevant care
- Household cost and coordination
- Language and digital literacy
- Doctor opportunities
- Facility workflows
- Blood and community support

Our intended research includes people from cities and underserved areas, medical professionals, students, fresh graduates and non-medical community members.

Survey findings should be published with their response counts, dates, participant mix and limitations. Product assumptions must be tested rather than presented as proven outcomes.

## 🚀 After the hackathon

The hackathon gave us a starting point. The next phase must establish reliability, trust and real usefulness.

### 01 — Validate the need

- Consolidate survey responses and interviews.
- Test the main journeys with users.
- Identify the highest-priority problems.
- Work with clinical and facility advisors.

### 02 — Connect the core

- Build the shared backend.
- Implement authentication and role-based access.
- Synchronize provider availability and appointments.
- Add consent-based records and notifications.
- Strengthen error handling and reliability.

### 03 — Run a focused pilot

- Work with a small group of willing clinics and doctors.
- Observe real operational workflows.
- Measure usability and completed tasks.
- Test the doctor–facility opportunity model.
- Improve the product from evidence.

### 04 — Expand responsibly

- Introduce evaluated AI assistance.
- Improve multilingual accessibility.
- Support multiple facilities and branches.
- Strengthen community coordination.
- Develop sustainable partnerships.

A 6–12 month direction will depend on team capacity, funding, clinical support and what pilots teach us.

## 💻 Running the project locally

The products are maintained in separate project folders.

Open the folder for the app or dashboard you want to run, then check its `package.json` for the available scripts and package manager.

For an npm-based app with a `dev` script:

    npm install
    npm run dev

For a production build, where a `build` script is available:

    npm run build

If environment variables are required, follow the relevant app’s configuration instructions. Use placeholder values in `.env.example` and keep real credentials in an ignored local environment file.

Exact setup may vary between product folders.

---

## 🔐 Protect the people behind the data

Healthcare information deserves careful handling from the beginning.

- Use synthetic data in demos and contributions.
- Never commit patient information or private medical records.
- Keep passwords, tokens and API keys out of the repository.
- Ignore `.env` and secret-bearing environment files.
- Use `.env.example` only for safe placeholder values.
- Rotate or revoke any credential accidentally committed.

Production security and privacy controls must be implemented and reviewed before handling real patient data.
